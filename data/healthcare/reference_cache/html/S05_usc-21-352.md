# 21 U.S. Code § 352 — Misbranded Drugs and Devices

## Key Misbranding Categories

**False or Misleading Labels (Section a)**
Products cannot have labeling that is "false or misleading in any particular." However, healthcare economic information provided to formulary committees may avoid this restriction if based on competent scientific evidence and relates to an approved indication.

**Package Requirements (Section b)**
Packaged drugs must bear labels containing manufacturer's name, business location, and accurate quantity statements.

**Label Prominence (Section c)**
Required information must appear conspicuously, in terms likely to be read and understood by ordinary consumers.

**Established Names (Section e)**
Drugs must display established names. For Rx drugs, established names must appear in type at least half as large as proprietary names.

**Directions and Warnings (Section f)**
Labeling must include adequate use directions and necessary safety warnings.

**Drug-Specific Requirements**
Additional provisions address compendium compliance, deterioration warnings, misleading containers, dosage safety, color additives, and Rx drug advertisements.

**Restricted Devices (Sections q-r)**
Must include established names and relevant warnings in advertisements.

**Recent Additions**
Antimicrobial drug labeling, nonprescription drug contact info, REMS, postmarket studies, product identifiers, reprocessed single-use device identification.
