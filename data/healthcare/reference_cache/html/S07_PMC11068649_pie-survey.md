# Health Care Decision-Maker Perceptions and Trends in Preapproval Information for Formulary Decision-Making

**Journal:** JMCP, May 2024, Vol 30, Issue 5, pp 475-479
**DOI:** 10.18553/jmcp.2024.30.5.475
**Authors:** Brisibe, Hikmat, Hydery, Gorey, Boswell (Cencora)

## Key Findings (n=17 HCDMs)
- 47% reported PIE shortened formulary decision timelines
- 90% ranked timely clinical/economic info as top benefit
- 100% rated anticipated product placement vs comparators as extremely/very impactful
- 88% rated pricing/budget impact info as extremely/very impactful

## Resources Utilized
- AMCP preapproval dossiers: 88%
- AMCP PIE webinars: 76%
- Preapproval presentations/videos: 65%
- Posters/abstracts: 59%

## Therapeutic Area Priorities (2022)
1. Autoimmune disease (82%)
2. Oncology (59%)
3. Rare diseases (47%)
4. Multiple sclerosis (35%)

## Legislative Context
Consolidated Appropriations Act (Dec 2022) codified PIE into law, providing statutory authorization beyond 2018 FDA guidance.
