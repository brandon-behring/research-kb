# Payer Perceptions of Real-World Evidence in Oncology Decision Making

**Journal:** Journal of Managed Care & Specialty Pharmacy
**Publication Date:** August 2021, Volume 27, Issue 8, Pages 1096-1105
**DOI:** 10.18553/jmcp.2021.27.8.1096
**PMID:** 34337998

## Authors & Affiliations

- Diana Brixner, Joseph Biskupiak, Gary Oderda, Douglas Burgoyne, Daniel C Malone (University of Utah, College of Pharmacy)
- Bhakti Arondekar, Alexander Niyazov (Pfizer, Inc.)

## Abstract Summary

This national survey assessed how US insurance payers view and use real-world evidence (RWE) for oncology formulary decisions. Researchers distributed questionnaires to 221 payers through the Academy of Managed Care Pharmacy, achieving a 45.9% response rate (106 respondents).

### Key Findings

**Primary Uses:**
- 84.9% of payers employed RWE to support comparative effectiveness where "head-to-head clinical trials" lack availability
- 41.5% utilized RWE results to guide off-label usage determinations
- Most valued validation of National Comprehensive Cancer Network treatment guidelines

**Preferred Data Sources:**
- Claims data (79.2%)
- Medical records (68.9%)
- Prospective cohort studies (60.4%)
- Patient registries (36.8%)

**Barriers Identified:**
Majority cited insufficient resources, personnel, analytical capabilities, and uncertainty regarding value of internal studies

**Outcomes-Based Contracting:**
Payers expressed interest but reported limited direct experience; implementation challenges centered on value measurement operationalization

**Respondent Profile:** Primarily pharmacists (89.9%), mostly from managed care organizations and pharmacy benefit managers
