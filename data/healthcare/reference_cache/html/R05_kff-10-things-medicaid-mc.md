# 10 Things to Know About Medicaid Managed Care

## 1. Capitated Managed Care Dominates Medicaid Delivery
As of July 2025, 42 states contract with comprehensive, risk-based MCOs. "Nearly all states have some form of managed care in place." Research on MCOs' actual impact on access and costs remains "both limited and mixed."

## 2. MCO Payments Represent Half of Medicaid Spending
In FY 2024, MCO payments accounted for approximately 50% of total national Medicaid spending of $919 billion.

## 3. Enrollment Reaches 78% of Beneficiaries
As of July 1, 2024, over 66 million Medicaid enrollees—representing 78% of all beneficiaries—received care through risk-based MCOs.

## 4. Children and Expansion Adults Lead Enrollment
Children show highest enrollment at 90%, followed by ACA expansion adults at 86%. Other adults: 72%. Seniors: 43%. People with disabilities: 66%.

## 5. Five Firms Control Nearly Half the Market
Centene, UnitedHealth Group, Elevance, Molina, and Aetna/CVS account for 47% of all Medicaid MCO enrollment. Fifteen multi-state firms collectively account for over 62%.

## 6. States Carve Services Out of MCO Contracts
Common carved-out services include dental, non-emergency medical transportation, and behavioral health. Over two-thirds of MCO enrollees also use at least one limited benefit prepaid health plan and/or received fee-for-service care in 2023.

## 7. Rate-Setting Uses Multiple Risk Mitigation Tools
MCOs receive PMPM capitation payments. Rates must be actuarially sound. Average medical loss ratios increased from 88% in 2023 to 91% in 2024—the highest in the past decade. 33 MCO states require remittance payments when MCOs fail to meet minimum MLR requirements.

## 8. State Directed Payments Impact Provider Compensation
States may implement "state directed payments" (SDPs) under CMS approval. The 2025 federal budget reconciliation law introduces caps and reductions to provider taxes and SDPs.
