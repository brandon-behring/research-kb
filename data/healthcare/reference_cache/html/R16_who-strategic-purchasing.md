# Strategic Purchasing for Universal Health Coverage: Key Policy Issues and Questions

**Publication Date:** November 20, 2017
**Document Type:** Technical document
**ISBN:** 9789241513319

## Overview

This WHO publication examines strategic purchasing as a core principle in health financing reforms aimed at advancing universal health coverage (UHC). The document defines strategic purchasing as "linking the transfer of funds to providers, at least in part, to information on aspects of their performance or the health needs of the population they serve."

## Key Objectives

Strategic purchasing pursues multiple aims:
- Enhancing equitable resource distribution
- Increasing operational efficiency
- Managing expenditure growth
- Promoting quality in health service delivery
- Strengthening provider and purchaser accountability to populations

## Five Critical Policy Issues

The document identifies five key policy challenges requiring attention:

1. **Governance** - Establishing appropriate oversight frameworks
2. **Information Management Systems** - Creating robust data infrastructure
3. **Benefit Design** - Determining service coverage parameters
4. **Mixed Provider Payment Systems** - Balancing multiple payment mechanisms
5. **Managing Alignment and Dynamics** - Coordinating reforms across the system

## Key Findings

The expert discussions emphasized information management systems as "a critical backbone for strategic purchasing" and governance as an "overarching and cross-cutting function" essential for aligning strategic purchasing reforms with broader health financing initiatives.
