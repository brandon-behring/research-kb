# The Big Three PBMs' 2025 Formulary Exclusions: Humira, Stelara, Private Labels, and the Shaky Future for Pharmacy Biosimilars

## Key Findings

### 1. Formulary Exclusion Growth Has Peaked
Each PBM's 2025 exclusion list contains over 600 unique products. Growth rate has slowed due to substantial products already dropped.

### 2. Humira Biosimilar Market Transformation
- Original Humira vanished from most standard formularies
- Most of 22 marketed biosimilars now excluded
- Each PBM privileges its own private-label version:
  - **Express Scripts**: Quallent-branded (Boehringer Ingelheim/Alvotech/Teva)
  - **CVS Caremark**: Cordavis private label (Sandoz)
  - **Optum Rx**: Nuvaila-branded (Amgen)
- List prices range from 14% to 95% of Humira's list price

### 3. Stelara's Private Label Future
PBMs leading with private-label versions rather than manufacturer-branded alternatives. Stelara selected for Medicare price negotiation with 2026 max fair price at 66% below 2023 list.

## Market Implications
- PBMs' financial benefits from private-label align with plan sponsor/patient benefits
- IRA price negotiation could undercut biosimilar pricing strategy
- Biosimilar manufacturers face difficult choices about continued investment
- Convergence of PBM consolidation, private-labels, and IRA may suppress future biosimilar development
