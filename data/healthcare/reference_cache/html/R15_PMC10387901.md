# Payer Perceptions and Use of Value Assessment Tools in the United States

**Journal:** JMCP | **Authors:** Westrich, Hydery, Dharbhamalla, Buelt, Zheng, Loo, Graff (Xcenda / AMCP)

## Key Findings
- 86% of payers familiar with at least 4 of 5 value tools surveyed
- ICER and NCCN most useful for formulary decisions (57% and 49% rated extremely/very useful)
- Priority factors: appropriate metrics (92%), comparative effectiveness (88%), rigorous methods (86%)
- Lower priority: patient perspective (32%), provider perspective (31%), societal (20%)

## ICER Report Utilization
- 20% reported frequent influence on organizational decisions
- 59% occasional influence, 22% no influence
- Highest usage: high-cost drugs (78%), rare/orphan (71%), oncology/hematology (67%)
- Payers responded non-uniformly to identical ICER assessments

## Methodology
51 payer decision-makers surveyed (June-July 2022). Pharmacy directors (67%), medical directors (27%). Health plans (53%), IDNs (24%), PBMs (24%). Xcenda's Managed Care Network (310M+ covered lives).
