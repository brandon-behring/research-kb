# Payers Seeking Innovative Solutions to Control Specialty Drug Costs

**Published:** April 28, 2025

## Key Findings

### Cost Management Takes Priority
84% identified specialty drug cost management as top priority (up from 75%).

### Rebate Models Under Pressure
- 80% want to favor lower-cost drugs offering no rebate
- 60%+ interested in cost-plus pricing models

### Cell and Gene Therapies Present Uncertainty
- 73% expect gene therapies to pose moderate-to-major financial challenges within 2-3 years
- Nearly 40% lack financial protection products for gene therapy risk

### Data Gaps Hinder Strategy
Plans struggle with cost-sharing affordability and lack reliable data on medication impact.

### Utilization Management Carveouts Growing
- 9% of employers and 24% of health plans currently carve out UM services
- Additional 24% of plans considering carveouts
