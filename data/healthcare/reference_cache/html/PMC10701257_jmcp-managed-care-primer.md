# A Primer on Managed Care Pharmacy

**Journal:** JMCP, Dec 2023, Vol 29, Issue 12, pp 1371-1376
**Authors:** Laura Happe (AMCP/U of Florida), Babette Edgar (BluePeak Advisors)

## Definition
"The practice of applying clinical and scientific evidence to support the appropriate use of medications to enhance patient and population health outcomes while optimizing use of limited health care resources."

## Five Core Competencies

### 1. Pharmacy Benefit Design
Drug coverage scope, in-network pharmacy participation, member costs, clinical support. ~85% of Americans have Rx coverage.

### 2. Formulary and Medication Utilization Management
- Formularies: list of covered medications
- UM tools: quantity limits, prior authorization, step therapy

### 3. Clinical Program Development
- Target populations, identify care gaps, implement interventions, measure outcomes
- MTM (Medicare), DUR (Medicare + Medicaid) required

### 4. Quality and Safety Management
- PQA develops/validates quality measures
- Medicare plans report to CMS; commercial may seek NCQA/URAC accreditation

### 5. Promotion of Affordability
Network incentives, tiered formularies, total cost optimization, waste reduction, value-based contracting

## Emerging Challenges
Health equity, high-cost therapies (CGT), supply chain resilience, biosimilar access
