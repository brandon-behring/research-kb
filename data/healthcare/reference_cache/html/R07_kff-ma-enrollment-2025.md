# Medicare Advantage in 2025: Enrollment Update and Key Trends

## Key Findings for 2025

### Enrollment Growth
- **54% of eligible Medicare beneficiaries** (34.1M of 62.8M) enrolled in MA in 2025
- Growth of 1.3M beneficiaries (4%), slower than prior year's 7%
- Up from 19% in 2007

### Plan Type Distribution
- Individual plans: 62% (21.2M), declining from 71% in 2010
- Employer/union group plans: 17% (5.7M), flat year-over-year
- Special Needs Plans (SNPs): 21%, up from 14% in 2020

### Special Needs Plans Breakdown
- **Dual-eligible (D-SNPs):** 6.1M enrollees (83% of SNPs)
- **Chronic condition (C-SNPs):** 1.2M enrollees (16%), 71% increase YoY
- **Institutional (I-SNPs):** 115K enrollees (2%)

## Market Concentration

| Parent Organization | Enrollment | Market Share |
|---|---|---|
| UnitedHealth Group | 9.9M | 29% |
| Humana Inc. | 5.7M | 17% |
| CVS Health Corporation | 4.1M | 12% |
| Elevance Health Inc. | 2.2M | 7% |
| Kaiser Foundation | 2.0M | 6% |

UnitedHealth and Humana together: 46% of all MA enrollees. In ~26% of counties (815), these two control at least 75%.

## Financial Impact
MA payments are 20% more per person than traditional Medicare, translating to ~$84B in additional federal spending annually (vs $18B a decade ago).

## Projections
CBO projects MA enrollment reaching 64% by 2034.
