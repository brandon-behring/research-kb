# Medicaid Managed Care Reporting and Transparency: Managed Care Program Annual Reports

## Introduction

Comprehensive, capitated managed care dominates Medicaid, serving approximately 78% of beneficiaries (over 66 million individuals as of July 2024) and accounting for 50% of total Medicaid spending (exceeding $458 billion in FY 2024). States contract with over 290 individual Medicaid managed care organizations representing a mix of private for-profit, non-profit, and government plans.

These managed care contracts rank among "the largest and most complex state contracts, frequently exceeding billions of dollars a year."

## MCPAR Background

The Managed Care Program Annual Report (MCPAR) requirement emerged from regulatory changes in 2016 and 2024.

**Implementation Timeline:**
- First MCPARs due between December 2022 and September 2023
- States must submit reports within 180 days after each contract year
- As of February 2026, CMS has made 2023 and 2024 reports publicly available

## MCPAR Data Elements

### Plan-Level Data Categories
- **Program Characteristics and Enrollment**: Plan enrollment figures, share of state's total
- **Financial Performance**: Medical Loss Ratio (MLR) metrics
- **Grievances, Appeals, and Fair Hearings**: Resolved appeals/grievances, outcomes, external medical review decisions
- **Prior Authorization** (effective June 2026): Total PA requests, approval/denial percentages, decision timeframes
- **Quality and Performance Measures**: Performance data across select quality domains
- **Encounter Data Reporting**: Share meeting timely requirements
- **Sanctions and Corrective Actions**: Intervention type, amounts, dates
- **Program Integrity**: Investigations, overpayment recoveries
- **In Lieu of Services and Settings (ILOS)**: Offered services and utilization
- **Patient Access API Usage** (effective June 2026): Data transfer metrics

## Key Limitations

- Direct comparison across states challenging due to inconsistent methods
- Low appeal numbers could indicate adequate access OR inadequate tracking
- Reports posted as PDF files, limiting accessibility
- Delays between contract year completion and posting
- Still in initial implementation years, template evolving

## CMS Plans for Data Enhancement

CMS developing "internal appeals and grievance dashboard" targeted for June 2026. Prior authorization data requirements expand significantly beginning June 2026.
