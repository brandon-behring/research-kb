# AMCP Real-World Evidence Standards: Overcoming Barriers to Using RWE in US Payer Decision-Making

**Journal:** JMCP, December 2025, Vol 31, Issue 12, pp 1230-1236
**DOI:** 10.18553/jmcp.2025.25108 | **PMID:** 40964890
**Authors:** Lockhart, Powers, Sweet, Gleason, Brixner (AMCP Research Institute / IQVIA / Prime Therapeutics / U of Utah)

## Key Finding
Only 18% of 36 surveyed payers regularly use RWE despite 80% expressing interest. Primary barrier: lack of understanding and experience interpreting RWE.

## RWE Assessment Criteria (29 Total, 6 Categories)
1. **General Study Questions** (4): Generalizability, settings, population relevance
2. **Data Source** (5): Source appropriateness, exposure ID, selection bias
3. **Outcomes Measured** (6): Definitions, comparators, validation, subgroups
4. **Analytics** (8): Statistical methods, confounders, economic perspective
5. **Results** (5): Consistency, significance, interpretability
6. **Limitations** (1): Clear descriptions

## RWE Framework by Product Lifecycle
- **Preapproval** (1+ yr before): Epidemiology, natural history, treatment patterns
- **Approval** (at launch): Pragmatic trials, external controls, indirect comparisons
- **Postapproval** (2+ yr after): Safety/effectiveness, meta-analyses, economic models
- **Indication Expansion** (1+ yr after): Comparative studies, cost-effectiveness
