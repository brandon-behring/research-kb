# Trends in Payer Reliance on ICER Assessments

*By Jenny Lee and Eric Latch — Published November 13, 2019*

## Key Findings

**Growing Recognition**
Payer perception that an independent HTA body is necessary in the US increased by 77% year-over-year.

**Widespread Familiarity**
Payers find ICER reports valuable for cost-effectiveness indicators, comparative effectiveness data, and value-based pricing benchmarks.

**Influence on Manufacturer Pricing**
Over 60% observed manufacturers proactively preparing for ICER assessments earlier in development. XELJANZ and PCSK9 inhibitors saw pricing adjustments following unfavorable ICER analyses.

**Practical Utilization**
Payers use ICER for literature reviews, negotiation leverage, utilization criteria development, and therapeutic class comparisons. Rebate negotiations based on ICER thresholds ranged from ~25% to 49% of price differentials.

## Research Scope
2019 study. 31 respondents (medical and pharmacy directors). Payer organizations covering average 6.1 million lives.
