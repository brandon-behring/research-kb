# A Guide to Approaching IDNs for Pharma and Biotech Firms

## Overview
More than 80% of hospitals are part of an IDN, making these networks crucial for pharma market access. IDNs centralize decision-making across multiple facilities.

## Key IDN Metrics to Research
- Firmographics: Location, size, ownership, decision-makers
- Clinical quality metrics: Readmission rates, HCAHPS scores
- Financial performance: Revenue, expenses, payor mix
- Physician composition: Specialties and provider volume
- Prescription/medical claims volumes
- Referral patterns
- Technology adoption
- GPO affiliations

## Crafting Your Value Proposition

| Priority | Example | Key Benefit |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Cost control | Reduced treatment costs | Lower acquisition costs |
| Patient outcomes | Superior efficacy data | Better clinical results |
| Readmissions | Reduced hospital returns | Cost savings |
| Operational efficiency | Simplified dosing regimens | Workflow improvements |
| Population health | Early disease detection | Preventive care |

## Identifying Key Opinion Leaders
Seek influential clinicians within target IDNs with relevant publications or significant patient volumes for your therapy.
