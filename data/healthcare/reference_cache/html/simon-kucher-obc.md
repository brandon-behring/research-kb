# Innovation is Revolutionizing Outcome-Driven Payer Contracting in US Pharma and Biotech

**Published:** June 2, 2025
**Authors:** Stephen Dunbar, Shannon Bermudez

## The Strategic Shift
More than 58% of US payers had at least one outcomes-based contract as of 2022.

## Key Drivers
### Policy Shifts
- IRA increases scrutiny of drug pricing
- Regulatory developments support RWE use in reimbursement
- Structural barriers being reduced

### Technology Advancements
- Enhanced outcome transparency via RWE and AI
- Automated validation systems
- Infrastructure for tracking agreement performance

## Real-World Success Cases
- **Cardiovascular:** 22% reduction in hospital admissions via claims data + remote monitoring
- **AI-Powered Asthma:** 28% reduction in ER visits, $6.2M saved
- **Digital Therapeutics for OUD:** Per-patient cost decreases up to $3,832 in Medicaid populations

## Moving Forward
Outcomes-based contracting transitioning from differentiator to standard baseline.
