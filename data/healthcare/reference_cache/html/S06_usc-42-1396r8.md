# 42 U.S.C. § 1396r-8 — Payment for Covered Outpatient Drugs

## (a) Requirement for Rebate Agreement
Manufacturers seeking Medicaid/Medicare Part B payment for covered outpatient drugs must maintain active rebate agreement with the Secretary. Effective for drugs dispensed on/after January 1, 1991.

## (b) Terms of Rebate Agreement
- **Quarterly rebates** paid within 30 days of utilization info
- **States report** units dispensed within 60 days by dosage form, strength, package size
- **Manufacturers report** AMP, best price, ASP, WAC within 30 days of each rebate period
- **Penalties**: $10K/day for late info; up to $100K for false info; 2x rebate difference for misclassification

## (c) Determination of Rebate Amount

### Single Source / Innovator Multiple Source Drugs
Rebate = max(AMP - best price, minimum rebate % of AMP)

**Minimum rebate percentages:**
- Before 1/1/2010: 15.1%
- After 12/31/2009: **23.1%**
- Clotting factors / pediatric-only: 17.1%

**Additional rebate** for AMP increases above inflation (CPI-U baseline from Q3 1990)

### "Best Price" Defined
Lowest price available to any entity, EXCLUDING:
- IHS, VA, DoD, PHS, covered entities
- Federal Supply Schedule
- State pharma assistance programs
- Part D plans
- Medicare coverage gap discounts

### Other Drugs (Non-Innovator)
- Before 1/1/2010: 11% of AMP
- After 12/31/2009: **13% of AMP**

### Misclassification Recovery
Manufacturers must pay difference regardless of intent. Secretary can correct classification, suspend drug, impose penalties.
