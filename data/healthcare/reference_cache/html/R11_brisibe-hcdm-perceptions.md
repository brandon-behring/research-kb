# Health Care Decision-Maker Perceptions and Trends in Preapproval Information Utilization

## Publication Information
- **Journal:** Journal of Managed Care & Specialty Pharmacy
- **Publication Date:** May 2024
- **Volume/Issue:** 30(5):475-479
- **DOI:** 10.18553/jmcp.2024.30.5.475
- **PMID:** 38701027

## Authors
Tamara Brisibe, Zade Hikmat, Tasmina Hydery, Claire Gorey, Kimberly Boswell
*Cencora, Conshohocken, PA*

## Abstract

### Background
Preapproval information exchange (PIE) between pharmaceutical companies and healthcare decision-makers has expanded significantly, though gaps remain in meeting organizational needs.

### Objective
The study aimed to assess how healthcare decision-makers utilize preapproval information and identify optimal resources for evaluating products before FDA approval.

### Methods
A double-blinded web survey was administered to healthcare decision-makers between May 16-23, 2022.

### Key Findings
- **Respondents:** 17 of 30 invited advisors participated, primarily from health plans (41%), pharmacy benefit managers (24%), and integrated delivery networks (12%)
- **Impact on Timeline:** Nearly half (47%) reported that preapproval information shortened formulary decision timelines
- **Top Benefit:** 90% ranked timely clinical and economic information critical for budget impact evaluation
- **Primary Resources Used:**
  - AMCP preapproval dossiers (88%)
  - AMCP PIE webinars (76%)
  - Preapproval presentations/videos (65%)
  - Clinical trial posters and abstracts (59%)

### Conclusions
Preapproval information access—particularly regarding place in therapy and pricing—significantly accelerates formulary decision-making processes.
