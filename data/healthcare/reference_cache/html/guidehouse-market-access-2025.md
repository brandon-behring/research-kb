# Rethinking Market Access as a Commercial Growth Engine for Pharma

## Overview
Guidehouse's October 2025 analysis addresses a fundamental misalignment: commercial, market access, and medical teams operate in silos despite market access decisions determining over 70% of revenue outcomes.

## Core Problem: Fighting the Wrong Battle
Traditional pharma commercial strategies emphasize brand messaging, physician targeting, share of voice, and demand generation. However, "if there's no access, there's no script." Lilly's Zepbound exemplifies this—CVS Caremark's July 2025 NDC block significantly impacted performance.

## Market Transformation
- **Payers as prescribers**: They determine market entry
- **HTAs replacing traditional regulators**: Regulatory approval means little without payer coverage
- **Patients as activists**: Demanding affordability alongside innovation

## The Solution: Structural Fusion
Merge commercial and market access into a unified go-to-market engine where payer value is "centrally incorporated into the brand strategy from day zero."

## Six Implementation Actions
1. **Redraw org charts**: Position market access as executive-level function
2. **Own the value narrative**: Market access drives positioning across all stakeholders
3. **Unify field teams**: Account-based pods combining sales, access, and medical roles
4. **Align KPIs**: Joint outcomes like formulary depth and patient persistence
5. **Deploy RWE as commercial currency**: Across physician, payer, and patient channels
6. **Redefine access talent**: Growth architects and dealmakers, not gatekeepers

## Strategic Imperative
Market access teams clinging to dossier-focused roles risk obsolescence as AI automates submissions. The future belongs to integrated organizations.
