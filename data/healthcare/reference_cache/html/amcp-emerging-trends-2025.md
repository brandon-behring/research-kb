# AMCP Releases New Report About Emerging Trends Impacting Managed Care Pharmacy For 2025

**Alexandria, Va., March 11, 2025**

## Key Findings

**Artificial Intelligence Integration**
92% of respondents said AI will be incorporated into more than 50% of prior authorization reviews. AI expected to strengthen cybersecurity protections.

**Drug Shortage Concerns**
62% said active drug shortages will increase by more than 25% within five years.

**Affordability and Payment Models**
54% believe at least half of states will form prescription drug affordability boards. 97% predict GLP-1 therapies will increase by at least 25% over five years.

## Report Details
Published in JMCP. Contributors from Prime Therapeutics, Aon, Biologics and Biosimilars Collective Intelligence Consortium, and academic institutions.
