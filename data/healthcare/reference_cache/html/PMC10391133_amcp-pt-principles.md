# AMCP Partnership Forum: Principles for Sound P&T Committee Practices

**Journal:** JMCP, Jan 2020, Vol 26, Issue 1 | **DOI:** 10.18553/jmcp.2020.26.1.48
**Authors:** Eric Jen (Precision for Value), Cynthia Reilly (AMCP)

## Committee Composition
- Administration, HEOR leadership, nursing, patient advocates
- Medication use, safety/QI, clinical specialties
- Periodic review of therapeutic categories as standard operating procedure

## Evidence Evaluation
RCTs and clinical guidelines most frequently drove formulary decisions across 17 payers (130M covered lives). Evidence considered: RCTs, meta-analyses, guidelines, economic evaluations, manufacturer dossiers, RWE, PROs, value frameworks.

## Key Best Practices
### Conflict of Interest Management
- Clear organizational definitions, standardized disclosure forms
- Regular reassessment (annual), tiered response mechanisms
- Compliance monitors during meetings

### Communicating Formulary Decisions
- Minimum communication standards, 5th-6th grade reading level
- Document P&T process rules, update rationale with each reassessment

### Inclusive Representation
- Processes to bring various perspectives at relevant points
- Chair responsible for facilitating diverse viewpoints

## Recommendations
- Include diverse expertise with designated roles
- Align on explicit value definitions
- Standardize and formalize processes
- Implement anonymous voting protocols
- Actively solicit patient perspectives
