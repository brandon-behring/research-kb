# Primary references

- **Time Series Analysis and Its Applications** (owned_indexed, core)
  Source: `fixtures/textbooks/migrated/springer_texts_in_statistics_robert_h_shumway_davi_nd.pdf`
  Notes: Entry point for the time-series branch.

- **Time Series Analysis: Forecasting and Control** (owned_indexed, core)
  Source: `fixtures/textbooks/box_jenkins_time_series_2015.pdf`
  Notes: Box-Jenkins reference.

- **Time Series Analysis** (owned_indexed, core)
  Source: `fixtures/textbooks/hamilton_time_series_analysis_1994.pdf`
  Notes: Deeper econometric time-series treatment.

- **Time Series Analysis by State Space Methods** (owned_unindexed, core)
  Source: `fixtures/library_books/James Durbin, Siem Jan Koopman - Time Series Analysis by State Space Methods_ Second Edition (Oxford Statistical Science Series)-Oxford University Press (2012).pdf`
  Notes: State-space and Kalman backbone.

- **Time Series: Modeling, Computation, and Inference** (owned_unindexed, core)
  Source: `fixtures/library_books/(Chapman & Hall_CRC Texts in Statistical Science) West, Mike_ Prado, Raquel - Time Series_ Modeling, Computation, and Inference-Chapman and Hall_CRC (2010).pdf`
  Notes: Modern Bayesian time-series reference.

- **Bayesian Time Series Models** (owned_unindexed, core)
  Source: `fixtures/library_books/Barber D., Cemgil A.T., Chiappa S. (eds.) - Bayesian Time Series Models-Cambridge University Press (2011).pdf`
  Notes: Explicit Bayesian state-space and time-series resource.

- **Applied Bayesian Forecasting and Time Series Analysis** (owned_unindexed, core)
  Source: `fixtures/library_books/Andy Pole, Mike West, Jeff Harrison (auth.) - Applied Bayesian Forecasting and Time Series Analysis-Springer US (1994).pdf`
  Notes: Pedagogically useful Bayesian forecasting text.
