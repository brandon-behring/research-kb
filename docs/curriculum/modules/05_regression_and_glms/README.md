# Regression and GLMs

**Module ID**: `05_regression_and_glms`
**Prerequisites**: `04_bayesian_workflow_and_model_checks`
**Goal**: Build linear, logistic, and Poisson modeling fluency with uncertainty-aware interpretation and predictive checks.

## Primary materials

- **Regression and Other Stories**: Primary regression and applied modeling text. (`fixtures/library_books/Andrew Gelman Jennifer Hill Aki Vehtari - Regression and Other Stories-Cambridge University Press 2020.pdf`)

## Support materials

- **Regression and Other Stories examples**: Examples and datasets aligned with ROS. (https://avehtari.github.io/ROS-Examples/)
- **An Introduction to Generalized Linear Models**: Support text for GLM structure and interpretation. (`fixtures/library_books/(Chapman & Hall Statistics Texts) Annette J. Dobson_ Adrian G Barnett - An Introduction to Generalized Linear Models-CRC Press (2018).pdf`)
- **Applied Logistic Regression**: Binary outcome modeling support text. (`fixtures/library_books/(Wiley Series in Probability and Statistics) David W. Hosmer, Stanley Lemeshow, Rodney X. Sturdivant (auth.), Walter A. Shewhart, Samuel S. Wilks (eds.) - Applied Logistic Regression-Wiley (2013).pdf`)
- **Logistic Regression: A Self-Learning Text**: Teaching-friendly logistic backup. (`fixtures/library_books/(Statistics for Biology and Health) David G. Kleinbaum, Mitchel Klein (auth.) - Logistic Regression_ A Self-Learning Text-Springer-Verlag New York (2010).pdf`)
- **PyMC examples**: Runnable patterns for the primary stack. (https://github.com/pymc-devs/pymc-examples)
