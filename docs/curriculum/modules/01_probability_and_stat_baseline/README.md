# Probability and Statistical Baseline

**Module ID**: `01_probability_and_stat_baseline`
**Prerequisites**: `00_inventory_and_prereqs`
**Goal**: Establish conditioning, likelihood, estimation, uncertainty, and simulation literacy before introducing Bayes-specific machinery.

## Primary materials

- **OpenIntro Statistics**: Fast refresh on sampling, estimators, uncertainty, and applied statistical literacy. (`fixtures/textbooks/migrated/openintro-statistics_nd.pdf`)
- **All of Statistics**: Compact mathematical baseline for likelihood, inference, and asymptotics. (`fixtures/textbooks/migrated/springer_texts_in_statistics_larry_wasserman_-_all_nd.pdf`)

## Support materials

- **Probability and Statistical Inference**: Good frequentist contrast text for the foundations phase. (`fixtures/library_books/(10) by Robert V. Hogg, Elliot Tanis, Dale Zimmerman - Probability and Statistical Inference (10th Edition) (2019).pdf`)
- **Introduction to Mathematical Statistics**: Use selectively for estimator properties and likelihood derivations. (`fixtures/library_books/Hogg RV McKean JW Craig AT - Introduction to mathematical statistics-Pearson 2019.pdf`)
