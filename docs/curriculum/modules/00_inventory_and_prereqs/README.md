# Inventory and Prerequisites

**Module ID**: `00_inventory_and_prereqs`
**Prerequisites**: `<start>`
**Goal**: Normalize the curriculum inventory, mark source states, and align the learning path with prerequisites rather than shelf order.

## Primary materials

- **OpenIntro Statistics**: Fast refresh on sampling, estimators, uncertainty, and applied statistical literacy. (`fixtures/textbooks/migrated/openintro-statistics_nd.pdf`)
- **All of Statistics**: Compact mathematical baseline for likelihood, inference, and asymptotics. (`fixtures/textbooks/migrated/springer_texts_in_statistics_larry_wasserman_-_all_nd.pdf`)
- **A Student's Guide to Bayesian Statistics**: Best beginner-to-computation bridge in the local library. (`fixtures/textbooks/lambert_students_guide_bayesian_statistics.pdf`)
- **Bayesian Data Analysis**: Canonical workflow and hierarchical-model reference. (`fixtures/library_books/Chapman Hall_CRC Texts in Statistical Science Andrew Gelman John B Carlin Hal S Stern David B Dunson Aki Vehtari Donald B Rubin - Bayesian Data Analysis-Chapman and Hall_CRC 2014.pdf`)
- **Regression and Other Stories**: Primary regression and applied modeling text. (`fixtures/library_books/Andrew Gelman Jennifer Hill Aki Vehtari - Regression and Other Stories-Cambridge University Press 2020.pdf`)
- **Time Series Analysis and Its Applications**: Entry point for the time-series branch. (`fixtures/textbooks/migrated/springer_texts_in_statistics_robert_h_shumway_davi_nd.pdf`)

## Support materials

- **Probability and Statistical Inference**: Good frequentist contrast text for the foundations phase. (`fixtures/library_books/(10) by Robert V. Hogg, Elliot Tanis, Dale Zimmerman - Probability and Statistical Inference (10th Edition) (2019).pdf`)
- **Introduction to Mathematical Statistics**: Use selectively for estimator properties and likelihood derivations. (`fixtures/library_books/Hogg RV McKean JW Craig AT - Introduction to mathematical statistics-Pearson 2019.pdf`)
- **Multilevel Analysis**: Support text for partial pooling and multilevel structure. (`fixtures/textbooks/migrated/quantitative_methodology_joop_j_hox_mirjam_moerbee_nd.pdf`)

## Optional materials

- **Bayesian Optimization in Action**: Useful, but not part of the trunk path. (`Documents/Manning_Books_research_kb/Bayesian Optimization in Action/Bayesian_Optimization_in_Action.pdf`)
- **Monte Carlo Methods in Financial Engineering**: Advanced simulation reference, not trunk material. (`fixtures/textbooks/glasserman_monte_carlo_methods_2003.pdf`)
