# Bayesian Acquisition List

Verified: 2026-04-06

This is the focused list of Bayesian books worth acquiring for the Bayes/stats curriculum.

The key distinction:

- **Acquire** means add it to the curriculum if free, or plan to buy it if paid.
- **Do not buy** means you already own a good copy locally, even if it is not yet fully reflected in the global inventory.

## Bottom line

You do **not** need to buy many Bayesian books right now.

The best move is:

1. Add the free books first.
2. Buy only 1-2 paid titles that meaningfully improve pedagogy.
3. Do not rebuy core texts you already own.

## Acquire now: free

These are high-value additions with official free access.

| Priority | Title | Status | Why add it | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Think Bayes 2** | Free | Best computational Bayes starter with notebooks; ideal bridge into PyMC-style workflow | <https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkBayes2/> |
| 1 | **Bayes Rules!** | Free online, print optional | Friendly applied Bayes text with strong pedagogical sequencing | <https://www.bayesrulesbook.com/> |
| 2 | **Probabilistic Machine Learning: Advanced Topics** | Free draft PDF | Strong advanced continuation for VI, latent-variable models, and modern probabilistic ML | <https://probml.github.io/pml-book/book2.html> |
| 2 | **Forecasting: Principles and Practice (3rd ed.)** | Free online | Best free forecasting text for the time-series branch | <https://otexts.com/fpp3/third-edition.html> |

## Buy if you want more pedagogical depth

These are the only Bayesian books I would seriously consider buying soon.

| Priority | Title | Paid? | Why buy it | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Statistical Rethinking, 2nd ed.** | Yes | Best intuition-building Bayesian text; much easier entry than jumping straight into BDA | <https://xcelab.net/rm/> |
| 2 | **Doing Bayesian Data Analysis** | Yes | Strong beginner-to-intermediate bridge with worked examples, software guidance, and videos | <https://sites.google.com/site/doingbayesiandataanalysis/> |
| 3 | **Bayesian Analysis with Python, 3rd ed.** | Yes | Best buy only if you specifically want a Python-native Bayes book rather than a theory-first text | <https://www.packtpub.com/en-us/product/bayesian-analysis-with-python-9781836644835> |

## Recommended acquisition order

If you want the shortest correct answer:

1. Add `Think Bayes 2`
2. Add `Bayes Rules!`
3. Add `Probabilistic Machine Learning: Advanced Topics`
4. Add `Forecasting: Principles and Practice`
5. Buy `Statistical Rethinking`
6. Buy `Doing Bayesian Data Analysis`
7. Only then consider `Bayesian Analysis with Python`

## Why this list is short

Your local library already covers the canonical Bayesian core better than the original inventory suggested. The real gap is not “missing Bayes books,” it is “owned Bayes books not yet normalized into the curriculum and learning path.”

So the acquisition strategy should stay conservative:

- prefer free official books first
- buy only the texts that clearly improve pedagogy
- avoid rebuying books already owned
